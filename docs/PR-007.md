# PR-007: v0.4.1 middle navigation and reading fit controls

Date: 2026-10-11
Status: implemented; automated verification passed.

Confirmed:
- Both normal canvas and comic reading support browser-style auto-scroll: hold middle at least 0.5 seconds, release, move away from the press origin to scroll faster, press middle again to stop.
- Comic right-click fits clicked/current image proportionally to viewport width or height, without changing original layout data.
- Separate global persistent wheel scrolling/zooming multipliers, default 1×, adjustable 0.1–5×.

Behavior:
- Small dead zone around a visible origin marker; both axes including diagonals; frame-time-based movement with a speed cap.
- Retain ordinary short middle drag. Cancel on focus/pointer loss, menus/modals and tab/mode switches. Cancelling by middle press cannot immediately re-arm on its release.
- Scroll multiplier affects comic wheel scrolling; zoom multiplier affects normal wheel and comic Ctrl+wheel. Auto-scroll is distance based.
- Image menu fits the clicked page; blank menu fits current reading page. Use a small viewport margin and preserve aspect ratio.
- Keep bilingual UI, settings migration, saves, source deletion and reading layout restoration.
